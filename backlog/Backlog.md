- [ ] **0.1.d2b — Grid areas: check the other 13 AT areas against the 2026 price sheets** `[data]` *(left from `0.1.d2`, decided 2026-10-31: only AT-VBG was completed in `0.1.d2`)*: complete Burgenland, Kärnten, Klagenfurt, Niederösterreich, Oberösterreich, Linz, Salzburg, Steiermark, Graz, Tirol, Innsbruck, Wien and Kleinwalsertal in the operator grid file (earnie-server `catalog/grid_areas.json`, checked with `python -m edibert grid check --require-check`): grid loss charge, grid base fee per metering type, the levy set `AT_NE7_LEVIES_2026`, `regions` (Kleinwalsertal and the city operators next to their federal state), network operator, SNAP as published absolute values where the sheet differs from the flat 20 %, and a `check` with date and price sheet. Corrections to the seed stay allowed under Earnie *Edibert P8*, but go through the operator file
- **Note (Earnie pin bump, `0.1.d2`):** Earnie must handle the grid-fee migration when it bumps the Edibert pin: a tariff with `grid_monthly_fee_eur` is `GRID_MONTHLY_FEE_LEGACY` on load; `api.plan_migration` / `migrate` move it only with `grid_fee_area` naming the user area of the house profile (several profiles with different areas or values, or a catalog area, are blockers), so Earnie decides the target per profile and shows the blockers in its migration gate. Its house profile and SE invoice consume the new grid-area parts (`GridFee.components`, `GridFee.fixed`, `PriceSeries.grid_fixed`) in their own step (Earnie backlog). Earnie's own work price ("Eigener Arbeitspreis") has no time windows while Edibert's own grid fee has; align both there
- [ ] **Earnie: share consent and exchange settings** *(moved out of `0.1.d` on 2026-10-31; was step S4; work in the Earnie repository, Earnie backlog "Edibert share consent")*: at the next pin bump Earnie asks for its own consent before any tariff is shared or use is reported, and only then passes an `ExchangeConfig` / contribution transport; no transport without consent
- [ ] **Simple tariff comparison** `[feat]` *(after `0.1.d`; decided 2026-10-31, free lead-in to the tariff comparison in Earnie; re-scoped 2026-10-31: logged meter data only, no standard load profile)*: yearly cost of the catalog tariffs and the user's own, computed on the user's logged grid meter data; result as cost per tariff with a hint to the comparison in Earnie (which adds PV, battery and shiftable loads). Takes over the `0.+1` item "vector multiplication"
  - **Input:** a CSV of the grid meter supplied by the user: grid import, optional feed-in. Same formats as Earnie's grid import (Earnie `docs/konfiguration/verbrauchs-csv.md`, "Bilanz Netz-Leistung"): one bipolar power series in kW (positive import, negative feed-in) or two cumulative counters `Bezug [kWh]` / `Einspeisung [kWh]`; Loxone layouts, `;` or `,` as separator, decimal comma
  - **No standard load profile, no annual-consumption-only mode** (decided 2026-10-31): an SLP-shaped profile says nothing about what a dynamic tariff gains or loses for this household, and comparison portals already offer that
  - **Checks (fail loud):** too short a span (decide: require 12 months, or allow a shorter span and label the result with the span instead of "per year"), gaps, duplicate or missing hours at the DST change, counter resets, ambiguous unit detection, negative import → error with the offending rows, no silent zero-fill. Earnie today sets a negative counter interval to 0 with a warning; the shared importer must not keep that as a hidden fallback
  - **Resolution:** keep the native resolution down to 15 minutes and aggregate energy-conserving to each tariff's slot grid. Earnie resampled to hourly so far (switches to 15 minutes with the move, Earnie backlog "Edibert meter CSV import"); hourly data would lose the quarter-hour day-ahead prices and the monthly 15-minute peak needed for the capacity charge (`0.+1` item "Capacity charge AT 2027", Comparison)
  - **Pricing:** energy per slot × tariff price at the real time of the slot, as in the invoice check; market-price tariffs use the historic day-ahead prices of the same period (`MarketArchive`), never a yearly mean; feed-in priced with the export tariffs; grid area and fixed fees included
  - **Output:** per tariff energy, grid fee, fixed fees (later capacity charge), feed-in revenue and net cost; the data period stated; for market-price tariffs additionally the average price paid against the period's mean day-ahead price (shows the effect of the household's load shape)
  - **Library location (decided 2026-10-31: Edibert):** the parsing, checks and conversion of meter CSVs move into Edibert as a module without Earnie concepts (no house profile, no upload directory, no Earnie-specific resampling); Earnie switches its imports to it at a pin bump (Earnie backlog "Edibert meter CSV import").
  - **Generic series importer (decided 2026-10-31):** the module reads any energy time series, not only the grid meter, so Earnie can use it for its load and PV profiles too (house total, consumers, PV, battery, Loxone energy monitor); Edibert itself uses only the grid meter. Layers: (1) reading: layout, separator, decimal comma, timestamps, header and unit detection; (2) series: power in kW or cumulative counter in kWh, sign convention, one or several value columns, 0/1 signals as raw values (scaling with a rated power stays with the caller); (3) checks with a structured report (gaps, DST, resets, ambiguous unit), errors fail loud, the caller decides nothing silently; (4) energy-conserving aggregation to a requested slot length. The grid meter (import, optional feed-in) is one preset on top; role names and the balance derivation (total = PV + battery + grid) stay in Earnie; Earnie requests 15-minute slots Not a neutral package: Earnie imports Edibert anyway, and the `opendata` package (Research Items "Shared library (weather)") is about weather and market data, not meter files. License: Research Items "License when Earnie contains the library"
  - **Privacy:** the meter file stays local; never part of a sharing payload or a use report
